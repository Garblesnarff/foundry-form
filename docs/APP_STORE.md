## Description

100% local. 100% offline. 100% yours.

Your body is the workpiece. Foundry Form is the forge that shapes it — analyzing your movement in real time using on-device pose estimation, scoring your form against ideal templates, counting your reps, and giving you instant feedback without sending a single frame to the cloud.

Point your camera. Move. Watch the forge measure you.

**Real-time movement analysis, entirely on your device.**
- Live Pose Detection: 30fps skeleton overlay on your camera feed — no lag, no upload
- Form Scoring: 0–100% match to ideal form for squats, deadlifts, push-ups, bicep curls, planks, and golf swings
- Rep Counter: Automatic repetition detection for major exercises
- Session Recording: Record and replay sessions in slow motion for detailed form review
- Local History: All sessions stored on-device — your movement data never leaves
- Exercise Library: Pre-built ideal form profiles across fitness, rehabilitation, and sport

**The forge scores your movement in real time.**
Set up your iPhone or iPad to view your exercise area. Start the session. Watch the skeleton overlay match your joints and the form score update with every rep. When you finish, review your session in slow motion — see exactly where your form broke down and what improved over time.

**Built for athletes, coaches, and anyone who cares about moving well.**
The powerlifter who wants squat depth feedback without a filming partner. The golfer perfecting swing plane without a lesson. The physical therapy patient tracking recovery progress. The personal trainer coaching remote clients. Foundry Form is the forge for movement that is measured and intentional.
